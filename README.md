# RESONANCE Unfolded

Create a clean, modern, minimal one-page website for RESONANCE, a youth-led organisation based in Kraków, Poland, focused on youth work, international cooperation, and Erasmus+ projects.

The website should be short and professional, mainly serving as an online introduction to the organisation and its Erasmus+ activities.

Website Structure

1. Hero Section

Organisation name: RESONANCE

Short tagline: Mind · Tech · Planet · Society

One short introduction explaining that RESONANCE is a youth-led organisation working with young people through Erasmus+ mobility, non-formal education, international cooperation, and youth-focused initiatives.

Simple CTA: Explore Our Projects

2. About RESONANCE
Briefly introduce:

Youth-led organisation

Based in Kraków, Poland

Focused on youth empowerment and international cooperation

Main areas: digital wellbeing & technology, mental wellbeing, sustainability, inclusion, diversity, and youth participation

Keep this section concise and avoid long paragraphs.

3. Our Erasmus+ Projects
Create a clean project grid/cards displaying our Erasmus+ projects.

Each project card should include:

Project title

Erasmus+ action (e.g. KA152-YOU / KA153-YOU)

Topic/theme

Country

Status (Partner / Sending Organisation / Participating Organisation)

Make the project section easy to update as new projects are added.

4. Our Focus
Use four simple visual categories:
🧠 Mind
🤖 Tech
🌱 Planet
🌍 Society

Add one short sentence under each category explaining the focus.

5. Contact
Simple contact section with:

RESONANCE

Kraków, Poland

Email

Social media links

Design Direction

Minimal, modern, youthful, and professional

European/international NGO aesthetic

Clean typography and plenty of white space

Subtle animations

Responsive on mobile and desktop

Use abstract shapes or subtle visual elements related to youth, technology, nature, and connection

Avoid stock-photo-heavy design

Avoid overly corporate or complicated layouts

No unnecessary sections such as blogs, testimonials, team pages, or donation pages

The overall impression should be: a credible young Erasmus+ organisation with an international outlook, but still fresh, approachable, and youth-led.
